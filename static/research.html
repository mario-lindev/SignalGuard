What a sound can tell us

Machine sound can reveal a departure from normal operation. SignalGuard is a research prototype that learns each pump’s healthy acoustic baseline and highlights changes for human inspection.

Grounded in acoustic research

DCASE 2020 Task 2 and the MIMII dataset study machine sound anomaly detection, including pumps. Low-cost pump sensing research investigates cavitation detection in centrifugal irrigation pumps.

What remains unproven

These studies do not establish that this app can reliably detect early failure in rural pumps from phone audio. SignalGuard has no measured field accuracy or validated warning lead time. Wind, phone placement, pump load and microphone differences can all change the score.

How this prototype learns

At least five verified healthy recordings train a statistical baseline for a single pump. Energy, zero crossings and six spectral measurements are compared using standardized distance. A score below 2 is within baseline; 2 to below 3 prompts watching; 3 or above flags a changed sound. These thresholds are provisional and are not probabilities.

Use the result as a prompt to inspect

Repeat a changed recording under the same conditions and contact a technician if it persists. Continue physical inspection, scheduled maintenance and water-quality testing regardless of the result. Full research notes and an evaluation protocol are included in docs/RESEARCH.md in the repository.
